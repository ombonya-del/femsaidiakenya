Privacy Policy

FemSaidia Kenya — last updated 1 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data the FemSaidia Kenya platform collects, why, how we protect it, and the rights you have over it. It covers the FemSaidia website and all of its tools — the main dashboard, hepa (personal safety), Red Flag, the case tracker, the admin portal, and related services.

FemSaidia works with survivors of and people at risk of gender-based violence. We collect as little personal data as possible, and we treat everything you share with extra care. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Your rights).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Community Media Trust, P.O. Box 9190–00300, Kirichwa Road, ADK House 1, Kilimani, Nairobi, Kenya. For any privacy question or request, contact halafu@femsaidiakenya.org.

We process personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and, where applicable, the EU/UK GDPR.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Where your data lives and who can see it

Data is stored in Supabase (a managed PostgreSQL database) and served via Vercel. Access is restricted by row-level security: sensitive reports and safety data can be written by you but are readable only by authorised FemSaidia staff, never by the public. Notification emails are sent via Resend. Public news articles (not your personal data) are classified for our intelligence brief using Anthropic’s API — no report or survivor data is ever sent to it.

4. On-device data

Some information — such as your hepa code word, check-in timer settings, and language preference — is stored only in your browser (local storage) and never leaves your device.

5. How long we keep it

Reports and safety records are kept only as long as needed for the purpose you shared them, or as required by law, and are deleted on request. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics may be retained for advocacy. Stale push subscriptions are pruned automatically.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you can ask us to: access the data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to its use; and receive a copy. To exercise any of these, email halafu@femsaidiakenya.org. We respond within the timelines set by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya (odpc.go.ke).

7. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, database row-level security, restricted admin access, and modern security headers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to minimise what we collect in the first place. If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services or a GBV helpline — hepa is a tool, not a substitute for emergency response.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted here with a new date.
